2. Nature of Data Collection (Public Data Ingestion)

Our ingestion engine collects exclusively publicly accessible, non-confidential data published on the open web. We do not solicit, purchase, or index private consumer personal data, non-public personal information, health records, or sensitive personal attributes. Our automated collectors query the following public sources:

  • Academic Research: arXiv API atom feeds across computer science and machine learning classifications (cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.RO).
  • Public Open-Source Metrics: Public GitHub repositories linked from published papers or tool announcements.
  • AI Products & Spaces: Public model cards, Spaces applications, and repository README files on Hugging Face and GitHub.
  • AI Startups & Organizations: Public corporate registry profiles, open-source organization hubs, and accelerator directories.
  • Technical Job Postings: Public career board feeds published on applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) by AI hiring organizations.
  • Industry News: Public RSS and syndication feeds from recognized technology news outlets.

3. Absence of Consumer Tracking and Cookies

The Platform dashboard does not utilize advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral telemetry. Session states and interface preferences (such as table sorting, pagination, and active category views) are processed locally in your web browser.

4. Source Attribution and Integrity

Every record presented in the Platform maintains a permanent, verifiable source URL pointing directly to the original publisher or repository. We do not claim ownership of third-party public content, and we canonicalize entity names solely for indexing, deduplication, and relationship discovery.
